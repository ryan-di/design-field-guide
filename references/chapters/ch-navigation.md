Navigation shows where you are and where you can go. It’s for places; actions belong on buttons.

## Entries

- [Side navigation](../entries/side-nav.md): A side navigation is a list of an app’s main places, down the left edge: Home, Documents, Shared, Settings.
- [Breadcrumbs](../entries/breadcrumbs.md): Breadcrumbs are a short line of links above a page title, like My drive / Family / Holidays.
- [Stepper](../entries/stepper.md): A stepper, also called a wizard, splits a long task into a few steps in a fixed order, such as Delivery, Payment and Review.
