> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

The work behind the principles, laws, standards and methods this book names, each with where the book uses it. The product examples aren’t listed: they’re this book’s own summaries, as [the preface](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#ch-preface) says.

## Part 1 · Good design

- [10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) · Jakob Nielsen, Nielsen Norman Group, 1994, updated since · article In this book: [Heuristic review](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#good-heuristic-review).
- [Severity Ratings for Usability Problems](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-to-rate-the-severity-of-usability-problems/) · Jakob Nielsen, Nielsen Norman Group, 1994 · article In this book: [the severity scale](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#good-heuristic-review-severity).
- [Good design](https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design) · Dieter Rams’ ten principles, Vitsœ · web page In this book: [Rams’ ten principles](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#good-rams-principles).
- [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) · W3C, 2023 · standard. Explained in Understanding [Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html) and [Target Size (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) In this book: [Contrast, and never color alone](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#good-contrast) and [Icon button](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#icon-button).
- [prefers-reduced-motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion) · MDN Web Docs · reference In this book: [Motion with a purpose](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#good-motion).

## Part 2 · Patterns

- [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) · Jon Yablonski · website, with pages on [Jakob’s law](https://lawsofux.com/jakobs-law/), [Fitts’s law](https://lawsofux.com/fittss-law/), [Hick’s law](https://lawsofux.com/hicks-law/) and [Tesler’s law](https://lawsofux.com/teslers-law/) In this book: [Lean on how people already behave](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#ch-laws).
- [The information capacity of the human motor system in controlling the amplitude of movement](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0055392) · Paul M. Fitts, Journal of Experimental Psychology, 1954 · paper In this book: [Fitts’s law](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#pat-fitts-law).
- [On the rate of gain of information](https://doi.org/10.1080/17470215208416600) · W. E. Hick, Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology, 1952 · paper In this book: [Hick’s law](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#pat-hicks-law).

## Part 4 · Effective design

- [Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup) · Ryan Singer, Basecamp, 2019 · book, free online In this book: [The loop](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-loop) and [Follow one problem through](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-worked-example).
- [The Design Sprint](https://www.gv.com/sprint/) · GV · web page; also the book *Sprint* by Jake Knapp, John Zeratsky and Braden Kowitz, 2016 In this book: [The loop](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-loop) and [Follow one problem through](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-worked-example).
- [Working Backwards](https://www.allthingsdistributed.com/2006/11/working_backwards.html) · Werner Vogels, All Things Distributed, 2006 · article In this book: [Write the problem brief](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-brief) and [Follow one problem through](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-worked-example).
