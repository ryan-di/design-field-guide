> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

The first minutes decide whether people stay. An empty page gives no clue where to start, what a finished result looks like or how the tools work. The [empty state](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#empty-state) in Part 3 covers the message on an empty screen. This chapter is about getting people moving: hand them something that already works, and teach the next step at the moment they need it.
