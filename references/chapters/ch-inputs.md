> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

Pick an input by the decision: does it apply right away or on Save, how many options are there, and does the exact value matter?

## Entries

- [Switch](../entries/switch.md): A switch turns one setting on or off.
- [Checkbox](../entries/checkbox.md): A checkbox marks a yes or no choice.
- [Radio group](../entries/radio.md): A radio group is a short list of options where people pick exactly one.
- [Segmented control](../entries/segmented.md): A segmented control is a row of two to five buttons joined into one.
- [Select & combobox](../entries/combobox.md): A select shows the current choice in a closed field, and people open it to see the list and pick one option.
- [Slider + number](../entries/slider.md): A slider sets a value by dragging a handle along a track.
- [Text field](../entries/text-field.md): A text field is a box where people type their own words: a name, an address, a message, a search.
- [Structured picker](../entries/picker.md): A structured picker lets people enter a value with a set format by pointing at it: a day on a calendar, a time from a list, a color from a row of swatches.
