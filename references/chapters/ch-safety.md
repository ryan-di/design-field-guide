# Chapter 9 · Scared to click

> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

When an action feels risky, people hesitate: they try fewer settings, explore less and learn the tool more slowly, because any click might cost them work. The patterns in this chapter all make actions safe to try. Undo takes the cost away after the fact, preview shows the result before it happens, and version history keeps a way back to any earlier point.

## Entries

- [Undo](../entries/pat-undo.md): Undo lets people act at once and take the action back afterwards.
- [Preview](../entries/pat-preview.md): A preview shows what a change will do before it’s applied.
- [Version history](../entries/pat-version-history.md): Version history keeps earlier versions of a document so people can go back to one.
