Click anything in the dialog that looks wrong.

  1. 1 · Visibility of system statusSeverity 3 · major

    A spinner and “Preparing…” don’t say what’s happening or how long it will take. People can’t tell slow from stuck.

    Fix: name the step and show progress, like “Adding photos, 3 of 12”. A progress bar fits here.

  2. 2 · Match between the system and the real worldSeverity 2 · minor

    “Re-encode w/ EXIF strip (q=0.8)” is the system’s language. People can’t tell what ticking it does to their photos.

    Fix: use their words and name the trade-off: “Make photos smaller (lower quality, removes location)”.

  3. 3 · User control and freedomSeverity 3 · major

    Cancel is grayed out while it prepares, and there’s no close button. Once the dialog opens, people can’t back out.

    Fix: keep Cancel and a close button working at every moment, and let Escape close it. See Dialog.

  4. 4 · Consistency and standardsSeverity 2 · minor

    “OK” doesn’t say what will happen, and it sits on the left, where other dialogs put Cancel. People press the wrong one out of habit.

    Fix: name the button for its action, “Export”, and keep the button order every other dialog uses.

  5. 5 · Error preventionSeverity 4 · catastrophe

    The name field takes characters files can’t use, like “/” and “?”. Nothing warns before Export overwrites a file with the same name.

    Fix: catch bad characters as people type, and offer “final-2” when the name is taken. See Constraints.

  6. 10 · Help and documentationSeverity 2 · minor

    The only help is a tiny “?” with no label, far from the options that need explaining.

    Fix: put one line of help right under the option that needs it, and link to the full docs from there.
