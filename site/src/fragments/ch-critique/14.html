SeverityMeansExample
0 · Not a problemNot a usability problem after all. Drop it and write down why.“I’d make it blue”, with no problem behind it
1 · CosmeticFix it if there’s time left.A typo: “Delivey fee”
2 · MinorLow priority: people notice it but get past it.Prices are low contrast

4 · CatastropheFix it before release: people can’t finish the task.Card payment fails if the address has an accent
