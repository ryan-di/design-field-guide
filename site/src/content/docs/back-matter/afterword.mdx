<section class="dfg-chapter" id="ch-afterword">

The four parts meet in [the loop](../../part-4/loop/#eff-loop). You frame the problem and set success criteria with Part 1, explore and build with the patterns and components in Parts 2 and 3, and check the result with the critique and review from Part 1 again. What you learn then starts the next round.

To start, take a ticket you already have. Before you open a design tool or an editor, [frame the problem](../../part-1/problem/#good-problem-framing): who has it, what they’re trying to do, what stops them, and how you’ll know it’s better. Then, before it ships, go through [Before you ship, ask these](../../part-1/good-checklist/#ch-good-checklist).

</section>
