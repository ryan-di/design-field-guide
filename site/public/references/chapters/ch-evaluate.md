# Chapter 30 · Check it with evidence

Design Field Guide v35 (2026-11) · Part 4 · Effective design

Cite as: Design Field Guide v35, #ch-evaluate

> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

A change is done when you can show it solves the problem, [not when it merges](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-loop). Pick the evidence that fits the change: people using it, a number from the brief, or a design QA pass.

## Entries

- [Watch people](../entries/eff-quick-sessions.md): Watching people use a design is the quickest way to see where it fails.
- [Measure](../entries/eff-measure.md): Measuring against the brief means choosing, before you build, the numbers that will show whether the brief’s goal was met.
- [Design QA](../entries/eff-design-qa.md): Design QA means checking a change everywhere people will see it before it ships: in light and dark, on narrow and wide screens, and in every state it can be in.
