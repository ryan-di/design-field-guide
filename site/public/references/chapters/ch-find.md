# Chapter 11 · Can’t find it

> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

A small product can be learned by browsing: open a menu, scan it, click. As it grows to hundreds of pages, commands and settings, browsing stops working, and people look for things with words instead: they type into a search box, or scan the labels for the word already in their head. The patterns below make sure those words lead somewhere: a palette that runs any command by name, a search that says what it’s hiding, and labels written in the words people actually use.

## Entries

- [Command palette](../entries/pat-command-palette.md): A command palette is a search box for actions.
- [Search with filters](../entries/pat-search-filters.md): Search with filters helps people narrow a long list fast, such as products, emails or files.
- [Labels in people’s words](../entries/pat-information-scent.md): People scan menus and links for the word already in their head.
