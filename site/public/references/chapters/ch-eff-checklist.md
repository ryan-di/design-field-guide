# Effective design, step by step

Design Field Guide v35 (2026-11) · Part 4 · Effective design
Live: https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#ch-eff-checklist
Cite as: Design Field Guide v35, #ch-eff-checklist

> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

**Frame**

- [ ] The brief names who, the problem, constraints, non-goals and success criteria. [Brief](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-brief)
- [ ] Agents get a context pack: tokens, components, copy rules, examples. [Context](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-context-pack)
- [ ] Done is written down, split into what agents verify and what I judge. [Done](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-definition-of-done)

**Explore and decide**

- [ ] I explored several directions along named axes. [Axes](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-axes)
- [ ] The risky part was prototyped in code. [Prototype](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-prototype-in-code)
- [ ] Directions were scored against the brief, not picked by polish. [Decide](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-decision-matrix)
- [ ] I checked agent output for things that only look finished. [Spot](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-spot-plausible)
- [ ] The strongest parts of each direction were kept, and the rest was cut. [Combine](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-cut-combine)

**Build**

- [ ] There was a plan, and each step was small and reviewed. [Steps](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-small-steps)
- [ ] Tests, affected end-to-end specs and screenshots ran. [Checks](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-agent-verification)
- [ ] Every visible change was my call, and the PR leads with it. [UX calls](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-ux-calls)

**Check and learn**

- [ ] A few people tried it, or there’s a plan to watch them. [Sessions](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-quick-sessions)
- [ ] There’s a signal for each success criterion. [Measure](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-measure)
- [ ] Design QA ran across themes, widths and states. [QA](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#eff-design-qa)
