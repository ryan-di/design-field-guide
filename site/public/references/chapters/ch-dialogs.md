> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

A dialog blocks the page until people answer. A side panel usually leaves the page usable, and only its modal form blocks it. Block the page only for a decision or task that has to come first.

## Entries

- [Dialog](../entries/dialog.md): A dialog is a box that opens over the page and asks the person something.
- [Side panel](../entries/side-panel.md): A side panel opens along the left or right edge of the screen.
