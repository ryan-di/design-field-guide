> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

An error is most frustrating when the product could have prevented it. The best error is the one that can’t happen, and the next best explains itself in plain words and loses nothing. Part 3 covers the parts that show errors, the [inline message](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#inline-message) and the [error state](https://ryan-di.github.io/design-field-guide/#error-state); the patterns below work around them, so fewer errors happen and the ones that do cost less.
