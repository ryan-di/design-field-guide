> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

The same product serves someone on day one and someone on day five hundred. Newcomers need few choices and an obvious next step, while experts want speed and control. Serve both with a calm surface first and faster paths underneath for the people who look for them, kept out of each other’s way. Nielsen’s heuristic calls this “flexibility and efficiency of use”.
