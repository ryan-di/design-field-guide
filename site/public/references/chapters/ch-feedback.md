> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

Feedback tells people whether an action worked. Match how noticeable it is to how much it matters: small confirmations stay quiet, problems stay until they’re fixed, and lasting states stay visible while they’re true.

## Entries

- [Inline message](../entries/inline-message.md): An inline message is a short line of text right next to the thing it’s about.
- [Toast](../entries/toast.md): A toast is a short message that appears near the edge of the screen after people do something.
- [Banner](../entries/banner.md): A banner is a strip across the top of an app or page.
- [Badge](../entries/badge.md): A badge is a small mark on another item: a dot or a number on an icon, a tab or a button.
