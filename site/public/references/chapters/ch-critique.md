> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

Taste grows by judging work out loud. Each time you say why something works or doesn’t, measured against what it’s for, your eye gets sharper. The three entries below are lenses for that judgment: a critique format that ties every comment to the goal, Nielsen’s ten heuristics for finding usability problems, and Dieter Rams’ ten principles for asking what good means. Try each one on your own work first.
