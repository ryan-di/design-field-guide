Every component spends time in states other than the default: loading, empty, error, disabled. Design each of them.

## Entries

- [Interaction states](../entries/interaction-states.md): Interaction states are the different looks one control takes as people use it: hover, focus, pressed, selected, disabled, loading, and the result after.
- [Empty state](../entries/empty-state.md): An empty state is what a screen shows when there’s nothing in it: no albums yet, no search results, no tasks left.
- [Error state](../entries/error-state.md): An error state is what a screen, or part of one, shows when something failed: a page didn’t load, a save didn’t go through, a link is broken.
