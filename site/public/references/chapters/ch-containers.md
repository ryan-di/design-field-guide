Tabs, accordions and cards save space by hiding some content until people open it. Keep in view what people need every time or need to compare, and hide only the rest.

## Entries

- [Tabs](../entries/tabs.md): Tabs split one thing into a few parts and show one part at a time.
- [Accordion](../entries/accordion.md): An accordion is a stack of headers.
- [Card](../entries/card.md): A card is a box that holds one item: a photo album, a home to rent, a product.
