> Evidence boundary: this is a learning guide. Its demos and product examples are illustrations written for the book, not verified product documentation, so check a product’s own docs before citing how it behaves. Tools named in it are 2026 examples, not endorsements.

Overlays sit on top of the page and close when you click away, so keep them small and never let them hold work people could lose.

## Entries

- [Tooltip](../entries/tooltip.md): A tooltip is a small label that appears when people point at something or move to it with the keyboard.
- [Popover](../entries/popover.md): A popover is a small box that opens next to the button people pressed.
- [Menu](../entries/menu.md): A menu is a short list of commands that opens from a button.
