The most prominent button should be the main thing to do on that screen, and there should be only one.

## Entries

- [Button hierarchy](../entries/buttons.md): A button starts an action: save, send, pay, delete.
- [Icon button](../entries/icon-button.md): An icon button is a button that shows a small picture instead of a word: a magnifier for search, an × to close, a heart to like.
- [Link vs button](../entries/link-vs-button.md): Links and buttons are both things people click, but they do different jobs.
